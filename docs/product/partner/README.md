# Product Requirements — Partner Application

The Partner Application provides business management and fulfillment tools for merchant partners on the **evrry** Super App platform, developed by **Elifsi Technologies Private Limited**.

---

## 1. Product Vision

Empower local merchants, restaurants, hotel operators, and gig service providers with intuitive tools to grow sales, fulfill incoming orders efficiently, track earnings, and receive transparent, timely payouts.

---

## 2. Core Partner Personas

| Persona | Typical Business | Primary Need |
|---|---|---|
| **Restaurant Manager / Chef** | Quick-service & dine-in restaurants | Fast order acceptance, kitchen ticket printing, prep-time setting |
| **Retailer / Grocer** | Neighborhood marts, specialty stores | Rapid inventory adjustments, barcode scanning, order packaging |
| **Hotel Front Desk** | Hotels, boutique resorts | Room allocation, check-in scheduling, guest concierge requests |
| **Driver / Rider** | Mobility & courier partners | Real-time dispatch requests, turn-by-turn map navigation, quick trip cash-out |

---

## 3. Key Feature Matrix

1. **Order Acceptance Console**:
   - Audio alarms for new orders.
   - Accept / Reject with delay adjustments.
   - Live order handoff to delivery partners.
2. **Catalog & Menu Management**:
   - Instant stock toggling (in stock / 86'd).
   - Modifier groups and customization options.
3. **Financial Insights & Settlements**:
   - Real-time gross sales vs. net earnings view.
   - Automated settlement cycle tracking and bank transfer history.
4. **Staff Roles**:
   - Separation of cashier/kitchen views from owner financial controls.
